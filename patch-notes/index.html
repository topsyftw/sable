Alpha patch log

Nov 17, 2026

SABLE Alpha Patch Notes: "Everything Goes Quiet Except Your Heartbeat"

Magic

  • New spell: Arcane Slash. It is bare-handed, costs 4 arcana, and costs 15 stamina if you are out of arcana. Drain your stamina low enough and everything goes quiet except your heartbeat. One more strike after that kills you. We are not being poetic.
  • Arcane Bolt is in. It has range. Your hands do not, which is how we got here.
  • Restoration is hold-to-cast now. Keep holding. The mint glow is not a suggestion.
  • The Q wheel and the hotbar now show cooldowns and arcana costs, so you can see the bill before you pay it.

The First Night

  • The new storm opening is live: your first night, the rain, and Lio on the trail. He was already waiting. The weather finally showed up on time.

The Map

  • The map opens in about a twentieth of a second now and pans instantly. It used to make you wait. It has decided to be helpful instead.

Loot

  • Dropping items works again. Use the Drop button, drag the item out, or press Z. The little thuds missed you too.
  • Small popups appear when you pick up or drop items, in case you watched something vanish and briefly doubted yourself.

Getting Around

  • The old menu harp is back. In browsers it starts on your first click, like it was waiting to be asked.
  • Y, middle mouse or the right bumper switches targets. Tab now only opens the quick menu. Tab had too many jobs.
  • Movement got an overhaul. You will feel it in the first few steps and then forget there was ever another way to walk.

The World

  • The game runs smoother. The forest and towns are about 18-19% faster. The trees have not issued a statement.
  • Loading is faster, and it's fixed. If an update breaks something, the game rolls back on its own and pretends the whole thing was a bad dream.
  • When a world fills up, it overflows into a fresh copy with the same weather and the same time. The rain does not start over just because you needed more room.
  • Text is easier to read. The letters were tired of whispering.

Coming Soon (Not Live Yet)

  • Ducks. They scatter when you run at them. They are not in the game yet, but they have been told.
  • Odo Tumbrel.
  • Elven fountains, which have been patiently full of water this whole time.
  • Starlit animations for the quick menu.
  • The Soulbound badge, for the things that mean to stay yours.
  • New storm sounds, so the rain can finally say something.

Nov 17, 2026

SABLE Alpha Patch Notes: "Please Don't Look Directly at the Shoreline"

Magic

  • Press Q to open a radial spell wheel for Spark, Frost, Growth and Magelight. Clicking way outside the ring now closes it instead of equipping Frost at a boar's funeral.
  • New spell: Restoration. It heals three hearts with a mint glow and little rising sparkles. It doesn't resurrect anyone, so please stop trying.
  • Casting at full health is free. That's not a bug. That's us respecting your anxiety.
  • Skill Sky moved to Tab, then down, then release. K no longer casts anything. K is unemployed now and taking it hard.

The World

  • Forests got thinned out, going from 1,436 trees to 755 in our test area. The other 681 trees moved to a farm upstate.
  • Shorelines are now smooth, curvy and natural instead of a staircase. Our artists cried. Our water also cried, but in a deeper teal.
  • Lakes have reeds, cattails, bank stones and reflected moonlight now. You may now stare at a pond for 40 minutes, as nature intended.
  • Grass no longer shows square tiles through its own corners. Grass is confident again.

Sneaking

  • Ctrl toggles sneak. You move at half speed, the screen goes dark at the edges, and a giant eye tells you whether you're Hidden or Seen. The eye has no further comment.
  • Enemies now remember where they last saw you for 2.5 seconds instead of forgetting you instantly like a goldfish. They still can't attack through walls, out of politeness.

Loot

  • Boars drop produce, seeds and fibre. They don't drop swords, furniture or cooked meals anymore. We don't know how they were carrying a wardrobe either.
  • Gilded Scamps still drop coins and stolen gear. They are thieves, and that's canon.
  • Furniture now actually stores things. Your bedside table finally has a purpose beyond holding up a candle.

Map

  • The map got a fullscreen glow-up with mist, fog banks, serif labels and a north arrow, for when you're lost but want to feel classy about it.
  • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and press C to recenter on your ivory little self.

Accounts

  • SABLE now asks "What should we call you?" Your answer is permanent-ish, lowercase and 3 to 16 characters long. Choose wisely.
  • The first player account, @top, is live and wears a teal DEV tag. Yes, the boss made his own account first. No, you can't have DEV. We checked.

The Gods' Emporium

  • A divine shop where you can try on the Gloamcourt Jester hat, rotate it and admire yourself. Checkout is still disabled, because the gods aren't taking payments yet. They're gods. They're busy.

Known Issues

  • Your friends may notice you sneaking. That's a friendship issue, not a bug.
  • Boars are still upset about the wardrobe nerf.
